Lipighor: 214 more free fonts

Lipighor is a Bangladeshi font maker with a big free collection, from newspaper styles to hand lettering. These are pictures of each font, because Lipighor's licence does not allow its font files to be copied onto other websites.

Before choosing one: Lipighor fonts are free for designs, including commercial work like posters and boxes. Using one as the website's font, or for company branding, needs Lipighor's permission first (see their EULA at lipighor.com). If the client likes one, we can ask them.
